While you are lost, some trivia.

  • The original Macintosh shipped in 1984 with 128 KB of RAM. Its Puzzle desk accessory is the sliding 15-tile game above.
  • HTTP 404 Not Found was defined in the HTTP/1.0 spec, RFC 1945, published in 1996.
