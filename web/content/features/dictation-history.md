## Never Lose What You Said

Every dictation is saved to **History** in Settings. The audio is written to disk before transcription starts, so a crash or a failed decode doesn't cost you the recording.

![VocaMac Settings showing the History page with recent dictations](/screenshots/settings-history.png)

## Find It, Copy It, Retry It

- **Search** your past dictations by text.
- **Copy** any result, or press **⌃⌘V** to paste your last dictation again.
- **Replay** the recording when audio is kept.
- **Retry** a transcription from its saved audio.
- **Original transcript** shows what the engine heard next to what VocaMac typed, with a one-line note of what changed.

## You Choose How Long It Stays

History stays on your Mac. Keep dictations for anywhere from one day to forever, decide whether to keep the audio recordings, delete everything in one click, or turn History off entirely.
